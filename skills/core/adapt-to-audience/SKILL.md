# Adapt To Audience

1. Identify the reader. If the request does not say, infer it and mark it `assumed`.
2. Set the depth for that reader:
   - Developer: mechanisms, code, and data.
   - Product owner: behaviour and business rules.
   - Manager: impact, cost, risk, and timeline.
   - Customer: what changes for them and what they must do.
3. Replace every term the reader does not use in daily work with plain words.
4. Remove content the reader does not need in order to act.

Output, in the user's language: `Audience: <reader>`, then the adapted content.
