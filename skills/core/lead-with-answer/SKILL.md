# Lead With Answer

1. Write the answer, decision, or conclusion as the first sentence.
   Do not open with background or by restating the question.
2. Follow with at most 3 supporting points, most important first.
3. Put details, caveats, and background after the supporting points.

Output, in the user's language: the conclusion sentence, then the supporting points, then the details.
